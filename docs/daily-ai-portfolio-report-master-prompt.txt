RSI INTERPRETATION — READ IN CONTEXT, NOT IN ISOLATION

Do not read RSI as an absolute number. Read it in context of the trend.

In a confirmed uptrend:
- RSI can stay above 70 for weeks — this is normal and is not a sell signal on its own.
- Only flag RSI above 70 as a concern if it is accompanied by bearish divergence, high-volume selling, or a major negative catalyst.

In a confirmed downtrend:
- RSI can stay between 30 and 50 for an extended period. A reading of 50 is not neutral — it may indicate the stock is relatively weak.
- RSI below 30 during a sharp flush may lead to a quick bounce, but RSI below 30 in a sustained downtrend can keep falling. Do not call it a buy signal on its own.

RSI divergence — flag explicitly when present:
- Bearish divergence: price makes a new high but RSI makes a lower high → flag as "⚠ Bearish RSI divergence" in the Caution column.
- Bullish divergence: price makes a new low but RSI makes a higher low → flag as "Bullish RSI divergence — potential reversal signal."
- Divergence is more actionable than absolute RSI level.

MACD INTERPRETATION

Always describe MACD in specific terms — never just say "positive" or "negative":
- MACD line above signal line → "Above signal line — short-term momentum positive"
- MACD line below signal line → "Below signal line — short-term momentum negative"
- MACD histogram growing in positive territory → "Strengthening bullish momentum"
- MACD histogram shrinking from a positive peak → "Momentum fading — watch"
- MACD histogram growing more negative → "Strengthening bearish momentum"
- Fresh bullish crossover (last 1–3 sessions) near the zero line → stronger signal
- Old crossover far above or below zero → weaker signal
Always state whether the crossover is recent or old.

VOLUME INTERPRETATION

Always compare volume to the 20-day average and describe it in these specific terms:
- Above 150% of 20-day average on a green session: "High-volume buying — confirms move"
- Above 150% on a red session: "High-volume selling — confirms weakness"
- Between 70% and 150%: "Average volume — normal session"
- Below 70%: "Low-volume move — low conviction, less reliable"
A breakout above a key level on low volume must be flagged: "Low-conviction breakout — needs confirmation."

CONFLUENCE REQUIREMENT FOR TECHNICAL READ

Do not assign "Bullish" or "Bearish" based on a single indicator.
The Technical Read label must be supported by at least two independent signals pointing in the same direction.
If only one indicator is available or signals conflict, use "Unclear" and explain the conflict in the Caution column.

Examples:
- RSI 66 + price above 50D MA + MACD above signal → "Improving" or "Bullish"
- RSI 66 + price below 50D MA + MACD below signal → conflict → "Unclear — mixed signals"
- RSI 27 + price below all MAs + MACD below signal → "Bearish"
- Only one indicator available → "Unclear — insufficient data for confluence"
